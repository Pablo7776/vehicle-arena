Settings + Menus  (version 1.8.1)
=================================

WHAT'S IN HERE
  addons/         the product. This is the part you copy into your game.
  demo/           a working example scene you can press Play on.
  tools/          the self-test that proves the pack works. Optional.
  project.godot   lets you open THIS folder in Godot on its own.

SEE IT RUNNING (about a minute)
  1) Open Godot. In the Project Manager, click Import.
  2) Pick the project.godot in THIS folder.
  3) Press Play.
  The pack is already switched on. Nothing to wire up.

PUT IT IN YOUR OWN GAME (about two minutes)
  1) Copy the folder  addons/settings_menus  into your project's addons folder.
     Make an addons folder first if you don't have one.
  2) In Godot: Project -> Project Settings -> Plugins -> tick "Settings + Menus".
  That's it. Anything the pack needs running in the background switches
  itself on. You don't add autoloads by hand.

CHECK IT'S WORKING
  Copy the tools folder in too, open  tools/settings_menus/verify.tscn  and press
  F6. You get a pass/fail list in the Output panel.
  tools/settings_menus/acceptance.tscn is the same thing as a big PASS/FAIL banner.

DOCS

  addons/settings_menus/README.md   what the pack does, at a glance.
  addons/settings_menus/CHANGELOG.md what changed in this version.

  INSTALLING.txt, next to this file, covers installing by hand, updating,
  using several packs together, and what to do if something goes wrong.
